## Before production

1. Change `https://www.varvellgroup.com` to the real domain.
2. Replace placeholder legal entity/address details with the real Luxembourg company information.
3. Connect `ContactForm.tsx` to your backend/CRM/email provider.
4. Replace stock image URLs with licensed brand photography.
5. Verify the varvell Group name, trademark and domain availability.
6. Add Search Console and analytics after launch.
